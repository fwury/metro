How to use Metro

Stations

  • Right-click an empty grid point to add a station
  • Right-click a station to edit its name, label side or marker, or to delete it
  • Drag a station to move it — its connections follow

Connections

  • Click a station to start a connection on the active line
  • Move the pointer along the grid to trace its path — corners are rounded automatically
  • Click another station to finish, Shift+click to finish and keep going
  • Right-click an empty point while tracing to drop a new station there and finish
  • Backspace steps back, Esc cancels
  • Click a connection to change its line, its order in a shared corridor, re-trace or delete it

View

  • Drag empty space (or Space+drag) to pan
  • Scroll to zoom, F to fit the map

Shortcuts

  • 1–9 pick the active line
  • ⌘/Ctrl Z undo, ⇧⌘Z redo
  • ⌘/Ctrl S save file, ⌘/Ctrl O open, ⌘/Ctrl E export
  • Delete removes the selected connection

Saving

Your map is saved automatically in this browser. Use Save file to keep a portable .metro.json copy, and Open to load one.
